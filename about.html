About Us

Rooted in the land since 2008

Who we are

Greenova began with one small plot and a belief that good farming should feed people without harming the earth.

Today we manage over 1,200 hectares and support hundreds of farmers.

Our History

2008 – The First Plot

Founded on five hectares of vegetables.

2015 – Expansion

Opened packing, storage and seed facilities.

2023 – Organic Certification

All main farms certified organic.

Our Mission

Produce nutritious food sustainably and share knowledge that strengthens farming communities.

Our Vision

A future where every farm is productive, profitable and kind to the planet.

Our Values

Sustainability

Community

Agriculture experience
